# 0042. Antenna variants, a heavy cannon and flared wall sections; log version 23

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-11-26
- Area: simulation, content, client
- Builds on: [ADR 0024](0024-resource-expansion.md) (sensors), [ADR 0032](0032-weapons-as-hardpoint-modules-and-the-shipyard.md) (hardpoint modules), [ADR 0036](0036-free-fire-recoil-hold-overflow-and-resource-density.md) (free fire, recoil)
- Log version 22 and ADR 0041 belong to the dock module (feature/dock-module), 21 and 0040 are the infinite rock field, 20 and 0039 the blueprint editor; this change takes 23 and 0042. If the dock module lands another number, this one is renumbered in the pull request that merges second.

## Context
The pilot feedback round ([spec, parts E2 and the weapons list](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-pilot-feedback-round-design.md)) asked for several kinds of antenna (the "antenna" part of the ship editing), a slow heavy gun with a big kick, and more variety in the walls of a ship. Until now there is one sensor kind with one fixed range; the scanner key has one fixed range; the navigation page lists everything revealed; the guns share one interval and the wall skins stop at six.

## Decision
- **Four new module kinds**, appended to `ModuleKind` (ids and content keys are the snake case names, the content hash changes only because of the new content):
  - `sensor_short` (80 cells, 5 power, navigation +60), `sensor_long` (520 cells, 18 power, navigation +260) and `comms_antenna` (40 cells, 12 power, navigation +700). The existing `sensor` stays (120 cells, now navigation +150). All four are one family (`ModuleKindIds.IsAntenna`) and share the research gate of the sensor (`Ability.Sensor`).
  - `gun_heavy`: 450 percent of the base gun damage per shot, recoil 4000 (at 450 percent a volley kicks about 3.5 times as hard as the mark 2), `cooldown_percent` 300. It is a hardpoint and a gun (`IsGun`), so it shows in the arsenal as mark 3 and in the shipyard palette.
- **Content fields.** `nav_range_cells` (antennas only) and `cooldown_percent` (guns only, 100 to 2000; 0 or absent reads as 100). Both are validated, hashed only when present, so content without them hashes as before.
- **Stacking with diminishing returns.** `ShieldSensorRules.Stack`: the best antenna counts in full and each further one halves again (second 50 percent, third 25 percent), independent of the order of fitting. Only powered antennas count (the structure's satisfaction must be at least the sensor minimum).
- **Scanner.** `ScanRangeCells(player)` is the base range (80, or 300 in the wide galaxy) or the stacked reach of the fitted antennas when that is larger, with the sensor research bonus. The scan command uses it. Passive reveal (`StepSensors`) works for every antenna kind with that kind's own range.
- **Navigation display.** `NavRangeCells(player)` is a base of 400 cells (above the scanner's, so a scan never reveals what the display hides) plus the stacked `nav_range_cells` of the antennas. The cockpit navigation page keeps deposits and threats within that reach (`NavList.Within`); a gate, a port and the selected row always stay. A world with the legacy flag lists everything, as before.
- **Heavy cannon cadence.** The volley interval is the weapon interval times the strength-weighted `cooldown_percent` of the fitted guns, so a heavy cannon fires a third as often and drags the rest of the battery with it; with only ordinary guns the interval is exactly what it was. Damage per second is a little better than a mark 2 gun, not a multiple: the cannon wins on the size of the hit and on the recoil, which is a decision (a ship that fires it must be braced).
- **Flared wall sections (client only).** A seventh ship wall skin, `SkinFlared`: stepped piers that stand wide at the floor and narrow upward on a plain section, built from the same constants the body collides with (`FlareStations`, `FlareDepth`, `FlareWidth`). It is a wall furniture skin like the ribs and the plinth, so no new section class is needed and every joint keeps its plate. The piers stay out of the viewport band and the stairwell; the collision is the foot of the pier (the deeper reach counts as solid, as for the plinth). Skins on station walls are not done (stations ignore skins) and stay open work.
- **Compatibility.** Log version 23 adds no command. `WorldRules.LegacyArms` (bit 6 of the second flags byte, off in every preset and in the new-game screen) refuses the four kinds (placing and the designer), makes the scanner use the base range and the navigation page list everything. Logs older than 23 are read with it on, so they replay as recorded; it hashes as default rules like the other legacy flags. The frozen test content (`content/legacy`) does not define the new kinds, so the golden checksums do not move.

## Consequences
- Benefits: antennas are a real choice (cheap short sensor for passive reveal, long sensor for scanning far, comms antennas to see more of the map), the heavy cannon gives a different feel, and walls look less alike.
- Costs: the bot never fits the new kinds, so the balance table does not move (the bot's scan range is unchanged because its sensors stay below the base scanner). A fixed navigation base range hides far rows for a ship with no antenna that the old page listed; the gate and the port stay.
- How it is checked: `AntennaCannonTests` (stacking maths and order independence, content and validation, scanner and navigation reach with one, two, three and mixed antennas, unpowered antennas, the scan command revealing a deposit only the long antenna reaches, the legacy flag and the placement refusal, old logs read with the flag, flag round trip and hash, replay and clone, heavy cannon cadence, damage, recoil and a mixed battery), `WallSkinTests` (the flared skin: footprint equals model foot, clear of the viewport band, joints between every pair), and the headless `Gallery --audit` of `ship-row`, `ship-big` and `ship-skins` (no floating part on the new models, no attachment through glazing).

## Alternatives considered
- **A. Direction-limited scans (wide against narrow).** The spec names a wide and a narrow scan; the simulation has no scan direction, so the variants differ in range, power and the navigation reach. A cone would need an aim for the scanner and is later work.
- **B. A new section class for the flare.** Rejected: the neighbour mask has no free bits, and a distinct section would need a plate for every pair of skins.
- **C. Slow every gun, not only the heavy one.** Rejected: it would change every old world; the per-kind `cooldown_percent` leaves the ordinary guns alone.
