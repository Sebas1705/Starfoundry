# 0032. Weapons are hardpoint modules of the ship, fitted in a shipyard's ship designer; log version 14

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-11-22
- Area: simulation, content, gameplay, client
- Design: [weapons and shipyard prototype](../prototypes/weapons-shipyard.md)

## Context
The ship mined and shot with weapons that were not part of it: one implicit mining laser (ADR 0027) measured from the ship's reference point and one implicit gun (`SetWeapons`), both free and fixed. The owner asked that weapons be real parts of a ship that is "completely modular and customisable": a ship mines and shoots with the weapons it carries, and a weapon is added or upgraded in a ship designer at a shipyard of the station.

Constraints from earlier decisions: the simulation is deterministic and integer only ([ADR 0002](0002-deterministic-simulation-core.md)); every change is a logged command ([ADR 0003](0003-commands-as-the-only-way-to-change-the-world.md)); old command logs replay as recorded and golden checksums do not move ([ADR 0027](0027-mining-laser-rules.md) legacy flag, [ADR 0028](0028-world-generation-obstacles-and-collisions.md)); a ship has a cockpit and engines and no core, a station has a core and no cockpit or engine ([ADR 0029](0029-docking-ship-and-station-are-different.md)); the ship turns about its seat ([ADR 0030](0030-ship-attitude-in-the-simulation.md)).

## Decision
- **Five new module kinds** (appended to `ModuleKind`): `MiningLaser`, `MiningLaserMk2`, `Gun`, `GunMk2` (the hardpoints) and `Shipyard`. All are optional content (`structures.<id>`; content without them loads and cannot place them). New content fields on the hardpoints: `hardpoint_percent` (strength: 100 for mark 1, 170 for mark 2), `hardpoint_range_cells` (lasers only: 10 and 14) and, for lasers, `power_use` (5 and 8). Mark 2 costs circuits and titanium alloy (their own research gates the materials); no new research node.
- **One catalogue, one rule.** `ModuleKindIds.AllowedOn(structureKind, kind)`: a station has no cockpit, engine or hardpoint; a ship has no core and no shipyard; everything else is shared. Both the single placement (`PlaceModuleIn`) and the designer use it. The hardpoints are fitted only in the designer: a single `PlaceModuleIn` of one is refused (`NoShipyard`) unless the ship is docked at a shipyard of its owner.
- **Mining laser per module.** In a world with hardpoints (`BeltNetwork.HardpointsRequired`: structure rules, a ship structure, content that defines the kinds, not `LegacyHardpoints`) a ship with no laser module cannot mine (`StartMining` is refused with the new reject `NoLaser`). A laser reaches a deposit when the deposit is within the laser's range of the laser module's world centre, which follows the ship's attitude ([ADR 0030](0030-ship-attitude-in-the-simulation.md)). Ticks per item are `MineTicksPerItem * 1000000 / (density * (100 + research bonus) * strength)` where the strength is the sum of the strengths of the lasers that reach the deposit: one mark 1 laser gives exactly the old number (checked), two halve it, a mark 2 gives 170 percent. Power: each laser that would mine adds its `power_use` to the ship's demand and is served before the machines as in ADR 0027. The ADR 0027 rules (trigger, research, brownout priority) are otherwise unchanged. There is no firing arc: the beam goes from the module centre (the game draws it from the tip of the emitter) to the deposit.
- **Guns per module.** `SetWeapons(true)` is refused with `NoGun` when the ship has no gun module. A volley picks the enemy nearest to any gun, every gun that reaches it adds its strength (`ShipWeaponDamage * percent / 100`) and the tracer leaves the nearest gun. One cooldown for the ship, as before. The ammo turrets in defence modules are machines inside a module and are unchanged; defence stays one kind (its tiers are the research bonuses of [ADR 0013](0013-defence-upgrades-are-research-bonuses.md)).
- **The shipyard and the designer.** `Shipyard` is a station module. `DesignerApply(Modules)` (tag 46) replaces the whole module list of the commanding player's ship and is accepted only while the ship is docked at one of the player's stations that has a shipyard (`NotDocked`, `NoShipyard`). It is validated like a sequence of single edits: the list is bounded (128) with defined kinds the catalogue allows on a ship and distinct valid coordinates; a cockpit and an engine (`NoCockpitOrEngine`), a reactor and a cargo module (`LastEssentialModule`); the modules face-connected, so the walkable path stays whole (`NotAdjacent`); research-gated kinds unlocked in a campaign world (`Locked`); the docked port module stays the same kind with its face free (`ModuleOccupied`); no module that holds a machine or belt is removed or changed (`ModuleOccupied`); the new hold still takes what the old one carries (`NoRoom`); no new module intersects the station or another structure at the docked pose (`Overlap`). It is all or nothing. `SimWorld.CheckDesign` gives the same verdict and the price without applying, for the UI.
- **Price.** A module that only moves (a removal and an addition of one kind) is free. Every other new module pays its cost and every removed or replaced module refunds half of its cost; the two are set against each other per item. The hold pays first and the station stock the rest (`NotEnoughItems` when both together do not cover it); a surplus refund goes to the hold. Sandbox and free-build worlds pay nothing. A module that moves or is replaced comes back at full hit points (damage is not carried).
- **Log version 14, legacy-gated.** `DesignerApply` is tag 46 and cannot appear in logs older than version 14 (the reader refuses it, also inside a batch). New reject values `NoLaser`, `NoGun`, `NoShipyard` are appended. `WorldRules.LegacyHardpoints` (flags bit 16, off in every preset and in the new-game screen): the ship has an implicit laser (strength 100, 6 cells from the ship's reference point, 5 power) and an implicit gun, exactly as before. A log older than version 14 is read with it on, so it replays as recorded. It hashes as default rules when it is the only non-default rule. The hash gains no new state: modules are already mixed. Tags 43 to 45 and version 13 belong to the construction rules change and are not used here.
- **The starter ship** (and the scenario ships) carry a mark 1 laser on the nose (module (0,0,-1)) and a mark 1 gun on top of the cockpit (module (0,1,0)), so the campaign plays from the first second; a station founded next to the ship starts 16 cells ahead instead of 8 (the nose module is there). The shipyard costs 24 iron plates, 16 iron ingots and 8 carbon, so it is within reach of the first hour; building one is an optional secondary goal of the campaign (`build_shipyard`), not a primary one.

## Consequences
- Benefits: the weapons are parts of a modular ship; more lasers mine faster and each beam leaves from its own module; the designer lets the player edit, move and upgrade a ship with the same module catalogue the station uses; nothing in old logs, saves or goldens moves.
- Costs: the starter ship has two more modules, so it is a larger target (the scripted player's win time moved, see the prototype); a ship that loses its laser module cannot mine until it docks at a shipyard; one command, one tag and three rejects for every consumer of the log; the content hash changes (old saves refuse to load, like every content change).
- How it is checked: `HardpointTests` (catalogue rule, no laser no mining, exact old rate for one laser, two and mark 2, power draw, lost laser, beam from the module with attitude, guns, hardpoints designer-only, codec and log version, legacy replay, key panel and strings), `ShipDesignerTests` (docked and shipyard needed, every refusal, free moves, campaign prices from hold and stock, upgrades, hold and overlap, clone and replay, the editor), the golden checksum tests on the stripped content (the new kinds and the starter ship's two modules are removed there), and the scripted player winning Relaxed, Normal and Hard.
- Not decided here: station-mounted weapons, a firing arc or a turret that tracks, ammunition for the gun, carrying module damage through a move, undocked refits, research nodes for the mark 2, defence module tiers, ship weapons for several ships.

## Alternatives considered
- **A. Hardpoints as module kinds fitted through one atomic designer command (chosen).** One kind per mark, the existing module grid and catalogue, one validation path that also prices the result.
- **B. Hardpoints as slots on existing modules.** Rejected: a second placement system and a second kind of state per module, while the module grid already is the customisation.
- **C. A designer made of many single `PlaceModuleIn` and `RemoveModuleIn` commands.** Rejected: a redesign would not be atomic, moving a module would pay and refund, and the UI could not know the price before applying.
- **D. No legacy flag: always require laser modules.** Rejected: old logs mine with no laser module and must replay as recorded.
- **E. A firing arc per laser.** Rejected for now: it needs a facing per module and a rule for the ship's attitude; range from the module already makes position matter.
- **F. Mark 2 behind a research node.** Rejected for now: a new node changes the tree the scripted player researches and every golden strip; the materials (circuits, titanium alloy) are already behind research.
