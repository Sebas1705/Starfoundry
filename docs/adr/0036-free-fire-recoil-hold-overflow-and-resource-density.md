# 0036. Guns fire along an aim with projectiles and recoil, a full hold overflows into ore pickups, and the galaxy holds more resources; log version 17

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-11-24
- Area: simulation, content, gameplay, client
- Design: [weapons feel prototype](../prototypes/weapons-feel.md); spec: [pilot feedback round](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-pilot-feedback-round-design.md) (part 1 sections W, S9 and M, part 2 section G)
- Supersedes in part: the "mining stops at 75 percent of the hold" rule of [ADR 0027](0027-mining-laser-rules.md) and the "a volley needs a target" rule of [ADR 0032](0032-weapons-as-hardpoint-modules-and-the-shipyard.md) (the rest of both stands)

## Context
Hand-testing the build of #82 found five faults in how weapons and mining feel:

1. The guns fired only at an enemy the simulation picked, so a ship with no enemy near could not shoot at all, and the shot was a line drawn to the target, not something that flew.
2. Shots had no force.
3. The mining laser would not break a rock while the hold was at the 75 percent limit, and the starter hold held 100 items, so one trip yielded at most 75.
4. Mined ore and drone output were far too low for a hold of that size.
5. The laser and the guns were switched with a stow and deploy step (`M` to take the laser out, `V` for the weapon group).

The simulation is deterministic and integer only ([ADR 0002](0002-deterministic-simulation-core.md)); every change is a logged command ([ADR 0003](0003-commands-as-the-only-way-to-change-the-world.md)); old command logs must replay as recorded and the checksums of legacy worlds must not move; the scripted player ([structure bot](../prototypes/structure-bot.md)) must still win Relaxed, Normal and Hard at about the same time.

## Decision
- **Free fire.** A new command `SetAim(dx, dy, dz)` (tag 59) sets where the commanding player's guns point: a direction in the ship's frame (the ship's attitude turns it into the world), each component within 1024 and a length between 512 and 1024; (0, 0, 0) clears it. With an aim set, an armed gun fires every `ship_weapon.interval_ticks` whether or not there is an enemy: a *projectile* leaves the gun nearest to where the aim reaches at the weapon range, with the damage the volley always had (`ShipWeaponDamage` times the summed strength of the guns). Without an aim the old behaviour stays: the simulation picks the nearest enemy in range and hits it at once. This keeps the bot, which never sends an aim, exactly as it was. In a world with `LegacyFeel` the command is rejected (`NotAllowedHere`).
- **Projectiles are simulation entities.** At most 64 live (`FeelRules.MaxProjectiles`), each moving 150 units a tick in integers for the weapon range (25 cells, 40 ticks) and then gone. Each tick a projectile's step is tested against enemies (a sphere of 300 units), the obstacles of its system (rocks, drifting debris, mines, wrecks; clouds are ignored), and the rocks of revealed deposits; the earliest hit wins (ties go to enemies, then obstacles, then deposits, each in id order). An enemy takes the damage; a mine is set off (its fuse becomes one tick, so the blast is the obstacle step's); debris takes the damage and breaks up at 40 (new obstacle state `Wear`, drifters may now be gone); rocks, wrecks and deposits stop it, with a spark event. Projectiles, aim and the new obstacle state are mixed into the checksum only when present.
- **Optional ammo.** `ship_weapon` may name an `ammo_item` and an `ammo_per_volley`; the shipped content asks for none, so a volley is free as it was. When asked for and the hold has too little, the gun does not fire and the game says so (`DryFired`, a click and a toast). Hashed only when set.
- **Recoil.** Gun module kinds carry a `recoil` content value (thousandths of a unit a tick). A volley adds to the ship's velocity, in the world and opposite to the firing direction, `sum(recoil * strength / 100) * 8 / modules` along the unit vector (`BeltNetwork.RecoilImpulse`): scaled by the strength of the guns that fired and by the mass of the ship (its module count against a reference of 8), capped at four times the top speed per axis. It needs flight inertia and an undocked ship; the engines brake it like any velocity. The main gun has recoil 0 (so existing play is unchanged), the Mk2 gun 3000 (a small kick), the mining laser none; heavier weapons are data away. *Not decided here*: a flight assist switch (the sim already brakes towards the target velocity, so a drift that the engines do not correct is not in the simulation).
- **Overflow into ore pickups.** In a world with the new feel hand mining no longer stops at the hold limit: past 75 percent of the hold it takes four times as long per item (a soft limit, so the factory keeps room), and when the hold is full the laser still works: the ore that does not fit becomes an *ore pickup*, a simulation entity (item, count, position, timer) beside the rock, merged per rock (so a toast says it once). A pickup drifts for three seconds, fades after three minutes, and is pulled in by a ship with room in its hold that is within six cells of one of its modules, and taken within two. Drones keep waiting at the old limit (they have no one to pick ore up). Pickups are mixed into the checksum only when present.
- **Regrowing deposits.** An emptied deposit counts down twelve minutes and then regrows 12 percent of its first size (at least 10 items), repeatedly, but only while no module of a ship or station is within its rock and eight cells; otherwise it tries again every second. The deposit accounting adds the regrown total back (extracted + regrown = mined). The timer is mixed only when running.
- **Resource density.** `WorldRules.ResourceDensityPercent` (50 to 300, default 140; presets Relaxed 150, Normal 140, Hard 120, Brutal 100) scales how many deposits the generator makes: above 100, extra deposits are appended after all the base ones (so base ids, positions and amounts never move), from a per-system random stream: more veins of what the archetype already holds (the home ones 30 percent bigger), and small ore-bearing rocks (a third of a vein) beside the solid rocks of belts and clusters. Every extra keeps 44 cells from the other deposits of its system, so the ground near the starting vein stays free for a station. A density of 100 generates the base galaxy exactly.
- **Content.** The starter's cargo module holds 400 (it was 100); the mining laser Mk1 is 250 percent and the Mk2 425 percent (they were 100 and 170); a drone bay takes 14 ticks an item (it was 36). The first wave's alert capacity is 234000 (it was 216000), which moves the wave clock back to where it was after the economy sped up (see the difficulty document).
- **Legacy flags and log version 17.** `WorldRules.LegacyFeel` (guns need a target, no aim, no projectiles, no recoil, mining stops at the limit, no pickups) and `WorldRules.LegacyResources` (no extra deposits, no regrowth) are new flags in a second flags byte of a new world rules format (version 2, written only when a flag is set or the density is not 100, so older rules keep their bytes). A log of version 16 or older is read with both on; it replays as recorded, and the reader refuses `SetAim` in it. A world with these flags hashes as default rules when nothing else is non-default (`WorldRules.HashesAsDefault`, `HashBytes`: a legacy world hashes the rules in the form they had before the density existed). Both are off in every preset and in the new-game screen.
- **Client.** `M` cycles the weapon in hand (the guns or the mining laser) and the left click fires it, in every world: the laser is in hand whenever it is the group in hand, so there is no stow or deploy step. The HUD names the group in hand and the key panel shows what the click does; a barrel along the aim (and a faint guide) shows where the nearest gun or the first laser points. With the guns in hand the click sends the crosshair ray converged on the first thing it meets (so the shots, which leave from a gun beside the eye, land where the crosshair is), an optional aim assist pulls it to an enemy within five degrees (a setting, off by default), tracers fly as the simulation's projectiles, sparks mark the impacts, the camera kicks with the recoil and the loose ore is drawn. The weapon card of the cockpit shows the mining rate and what is left in the deposit.

## Consequences
- Benefits: the guns work anywhere at any time; a shot is a thing with a path that can be missed and can hit a rock, a mine or debris; heavy weapons can push the ship; a full hold no longer blocks the laser and nothing is wasted by force; a trip is worth the flight; far fields and regrowth give the player more to find; old logs and the bot are untouched by construction.
- Costs: one command, two flags, a second world rules format and one log version for every consumer of the log; the content hash changes (old saves refuse to load, like every content change); the scripted player needed a pacing compensation in the content (the wave clock) and a sideways slide in its flight when pinned; the starter factory, whose pacing was calibrated for a hard stop at the limit, keeps that calibration in tests through `LegacyFeel`.
- How it is checked: `FeelTests` (the command and its codec, a shot with no enemy hits the first thing on its path, enemies, mines and debris, optional ammo, recoil maths with the attitude and with recoil 0, determinism over 5000 ticks and replay, a clone, a version 16 log, overflow only when full, the soft limit and drones, collection and fading, regrowth and its wait, density determinism and the base galaxy, rules codec), the existing golden-style checks with the new flags, the scripted player on three seeds and three difficulties (the difficulty document), and `--gun-shot` and `--overflow-shot` screenshots.
- Not decided here: missile launchers and a heavy cannon module (the recoil data allows them), a flight assist for linear drift, drones that pick ore up, the personal inventory (ADR 0035, part 2 of the spec) and whether pickups should follow the player's carried items.

## Alternatives considered
- **A. SetAim with projectiles; no aim keeps the old behaviour (chosen).** The smallest change for the bot and the old logs, one command, exact in integers.
- **B. Make every volley a projectile, aimed by the simulation at the nearest enemy.** Rejected: the bot and the old balance would move, and free fire would still need an aim.
- **C. A fire command with the aim inside it (tag 60).** Rejected: the arming and the aim change at different rates (the aim many times a second, the trigger twice), and a separate aim is cheap to throttle.
- **D. Surplus ore voided, or jettisoned as a command.** Rejected: it feels like a loss, which the owner said (the spec's option C); pickups waste nothing by force.
- **E. A hard stop at 75 percent kept for hand mining.** Rejected: it is the fault that was reported (the laser "will not break a rock").
- **F. Hand-mined ore that is past the limit always floats, even with room in the hold.** Considered: it would protect the starter factory better, but the player would see ore drift away with room to spare. The slowdown past 75 percent keeps the factory's room without that.
- **G. Regrowth without the wait for clear ground.** Rejected after the scripted player met it: a rock that comes back inside a parked ship pushes it away and the way back to the mining spot is blocked.
- **H. A density that only scales the amounts.** Rejected: the owner asked for more places to find ore, not bigger ones (the richness rule already scales amounts).
